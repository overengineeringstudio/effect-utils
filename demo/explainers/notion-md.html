notion md — your Markdown and Notion, finally in sync

Keep a folder of local Markdown two-way synced with Notion pages. No copy-paste. No clobbering.

You keep the same doc in two places — and sync it by hand.

You keep the same doc in your repo and in Notion — and there's no first-class way to sync them. So you copy-paste, they drift, and whoever pastes last quietly wipes the other side's edits.

Run notion md sync --watch — edit either side, both stay in sync. Live. Local edits push instantly; Notion changes sync back on a short poll (~30s). Two-way needs source: shared.

04…and it never clobbers

Both sides changed the same line? You get a draft, not a disaster.

local edit
Pricing: $25 / mo
notion edit
Pricing: $30 / mo
◆ guarded 3-way merge
vs base $20 · from .notion-md/
writes a draft →
⚠ product-spec.conflict.roughdraft.md
<<< local
Pricing: $25 / mo
>>> notion
Pricing: $30 / mo
✓ both versions kept · you decide

Every sync is a 3-way merge against a content-addressed base in .notion-md/. If only one side changed, it just applies. If both touched the same spot, notion md writes a *.conflict.roughdraft.md holding both versions — it never overwrites your work.

What first-class would look like: native conflict-aware Markdown ⇄ page binding, in Notion itself. Until then, notion md fills the gap.
